SMS Automation for Mac
======================

Install
-------
1. Drag "SMS Automation" onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "SMS Automation".

The first time you open it
--------------------------
This app isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first
time:

  - macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later: macOS says it can't verify the app. Click
    "Done", open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click
    "Open Anyway" next to SMS Automation, and confirm.
  - macOS 14 and earlier: right-click (or Control-click) the app, choose
    "Open", then click "Open" in the dialog.

You only need to do this once.

Using it
--------
- The dashboard opens in your web browser at http://127.0.0.1:47821
- Sending continues in the background when you close the browser tab.
  Double-click the app again to reopen the dashboard.
- To stop it, click "Quit app" at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Sending only happens while the app is running and your Mac is awake.
  To start it automatically, add it in System Settings > General > Login Items.
- Only this Mac can reach the dashboard.

Your data
---------
Settings, gateway credentials (encrypted) and campaign history are stored in:
  ~/Library/Application Support/SMS Automation
Logs are in:
  ~/Library/Logs/SMS Automation

Delivery receipts
-----------------
SMS gateways can only report "Delivered" if they can reach this Mac from the
internet. Without that, messages show as "Sent" (accepted by the gateway). Use
a tunnel such as Cloudflare Tunnel or ngrok, then enter its address under
Settings > General > Public URL.
